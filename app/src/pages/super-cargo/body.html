Montra Electric Super Cargo FAQs

What delivery range can I expect from Montra Electric Super Cargo when fully loaded?
The Montra Electric Super Cargo electric three wheeler achieves 200+ km certified range per charge for delivery operations. When carrying maximum cargo load (580kg in eCX variant), you can complete 180-190 km of delivery routes depending on traffic and stops. This electric cargo 3-wheeler provides consistent delivery performance throughout the day, unlike diesel vehicles that lose efficiency with frequent delivery stops and engine restarts.
What charging infrastructure do I need for Montra Electric Super Cargo electric three wheeler fleet operations?
The Montra Electric Super Cargo needs 4 hours 45 minutes charging time with standard electrical supply. For fleet operations, install dedicated charging points at your warehouse or depot with proper load management. This electric cargo 3-wheeler can utilize existing industrial electrical connections, making fleet electrification straightforward without major infrastructure changes.
What are the maintenance requirements for Montra Electric Super Cargo in commercial use?
The Montra Electric Super Cargo requires servicing and battery diagnostics scheduled as per your owner’s manual. Commercial operations benefit from 70% lower maintenance costs compared to diesel cargo vehicles, and up to 60% vs CNG cargo vehicles.
What business financing and government incentives are available for Montra Electric Super Cargo?
The Montra Electric Super Cargo qualifies for government subsidies as per national and state EV policies. Commercial vehicle loans, asset financing, and lease options are available with competitive rates for electric three wheeler purchases. Contact your nearest Montra Electric dealership for current incentive programs.
Which Montra Electric Super Cargo variant suits different business types?
Choose eCX electric cargo (580kg, open deck) for construction materials, vegetables, or bulk goods. Pick eCX d+ electric three cargo (510kg, 170 cu.ft) for e-commerce deliveries, pharmaceuticals, or high-value cargo requiring maximum protection and storage volume.
How much can my business save by switching from CNG to Montra Electric Super Cargo electric three wheeler?
The Montra Electric Super Cargo eliminates ₹500-700 daily CNG costs for delivery operations. Electricity costs only ₹30-40 for equivalent range, saving ₹450-650 daily per vehicle. Additional savings include 60% lower maintenance, no emission compliance costs, and reduced driver wages due to easier operation. Annual savings per electric cargo typically exceed ₹1.5-2 lakhs compared to CNG vehicles.
