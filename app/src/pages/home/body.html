Redefining Electric Mobility with Purpose

Montra Electric is a true-blue electric brand, focused exclusively on commercial electric vehicles to deliver smarter and cleaner mobility solutions. Our approach is built on maximizing customer business economics, ensuring higher uptime, improving asset utilization, and enabling consultative, solution-led engagement. Backed by software-led design and tech-driven insights, we empower businesses with efficient, reliable, and future-ready electric mobility.

Tech - Solutions

Driving Efficiency with Tech Intelligence

Montra Electric leverages advanced telematics, predictive maintenance, and real-time data analytics to deliver complete visibility and control to fleet owners. With features like live tracking, vehicle health insights, driver analytics, and early alerts, we help prevent breakdowns, improve utilization, and optimize performance. Backed by continuous monitoring and OTA updates, our ecosystem ensures higher uptime, better efficiency, and faster total cost of ownership (TCO) recovery.

A Legacy Built Over 125+ Years

Our journey traces back to the legacy of the Murugappa Group, a renowned Indian business conglomerate with over 125 years of excellence in engineering, manufacturing, and innovation. The group has played a significant role in shaping multiple industries across the country. Carrying forward this rich heritage, Montra Electric was established to lead the transition toward sustainable mobility. By combining decades of manufacturing expertise with modern electric vehicle technology, we are continuing this legacy—driving innovation, supporting businesses, and building a cleaner, smarter future for mobility.
